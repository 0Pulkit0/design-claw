# Phase 0.5 blends — capture list + draft precedence

Capture 3 blends, each 3-4 DELIBERATELY CONFLICTING components, each from a
DIFFERENT real site. Screenshot each component tight (macOS: Cmd+Shift+4). Drop
them in `crops/blend-1 … blend-3`. Name by role+style, e.g. `nav__brutalist.png`,
`hero__editorial.png`, `card__soft-saas.png` — I read them visually, so the names
just help both of us keep roles straight.

The precedence paragraph is arm A2's whole point — it encodes YOUR choice of spine
and conflict resolution. Drafts below; **edit the spine pick to taste before I
generate** (the spine is the biggest lever). The archetypes are suggestions —
grab whatever genuinely conflicts.

## Blend 1 — Brutalist × Editorial × Soft SaaS
Components: a brutalist NAV (hard borders, monospace, dense) · an editorial/
magazine HERO (serif display, big type, lots of air) · a soft SaaS CARD (rounded,
pastel, drop shadow). Optional 4th: a loud PALETTE block.
DRAFT precedence (spine = nav):
> The brutalist nav is the spine — it governs spacing, density, structural rhythm,
> and borders; everything conforms to its scale and sharpness. Take the editorial
> hero's TYPE CHARACTER only (serif display, generous leading) for headings, but
> snap its spacing to the spine's rhythm. Take the soft SaaS card's PALETTE only
> as accents — drop its rounded corners and soft shadows; corners and borders stay
> the spine's hard, square treatment. Resolve every conflict to the spine: density
> beats air, hard edges beat softness.

## Blend 2 — Luxury serif × Playful startup × Terminal
Components: a luxury thin-SERIF nav (whitespace, elegant) · a playful rounded
STARTUP hero (bright, big, friendly) · a dark TERMINAL/monospace CARD (green-on-
black, code).
DRAFT precedence (spine = luxury serif):
> The luxury serif system is the spine — restrained spacing, thin weights, lots of
> whitespace, muted palette. Take the startup hero's ENERGY only as a single
> accent color and a slightly larger CTA — none of its roundness. Take the terminal
> card's MONOSPACE only for small meta/labels, never body or headings. Resolve
> conflicts to the spine: whitespace beats density, elegance beats play.

## Blend 3 — Swiss grid × Neo-brutalist × Glassmorphism
Components: a SWISS/grid type block (Helvetica/neue-grotesk, strict grid) · a
neo-brutalist BUTTON/card (hard offset shadow, bold outline) · a GLASSMORPHIC
panel (blur, translucent).
DRAFT precedence (spine = Swiss grid):
> The Swiss grid is the spine — strict columns, grotesk type, a disciplined spacing
> scale. Take the neo-brutalist SHAPE only for primary buttons (hard offset shadow,
> bold outline) as the one loud element. Drop glassmorphism's blur entirely; take
> only its faint translucent surface tint for a single secondary panel. Resolve
> conflicts to the spine: the grid governs all placement; nothing breaks the columns.
